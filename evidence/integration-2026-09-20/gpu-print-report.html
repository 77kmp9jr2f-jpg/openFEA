Application notes

Use the unchanged built 40 × 10 × 8 mm imported coupon. Face 9 at X=0 is fully restrained in XYZ. Face 7 at X=40 receives TOTAL force [0,0,-4] N uniformly distributed over its area; all other surfaces are free. Replace prior bracket application notes and conditions. Print rotation is extrinsic X then Y then Z, R=Rz Ry Rx, mapping print axes into global axes; print Z is the layer normal. Force remains in global axes. Experimental directional homogenization uses illustrative pattern coefficients and bounding-box wall/skin estimates, not sliced infill or calibrated printed strength. No results or physical acceptance are asserted.

Astra setup assumptions:
- User explicitly adopts illustrative PLA properties and all listed print settings for software verification.
- Steady room-temperature, small-strain static loading.
- No self-weight, contact, bolts or preload.
- Print orientation and physical strength are not validated by this approximate model.

Assumptions and interpretation

  • Printed equivalent solid with directional homogenized stiffness; tetrahedral small-strain static analysis.
  • Fixtures are ideal fully fixed faces. Forces are distributed uniformly across the selected mesh faces in global XYZ.
  • No contact, bolt preload, plasticity, buckling, fatigue or thermal effects. Explicit infill geometry is not meshed.
  • Material presets are illustrative inputs. Verify properties for the actual alloy, condition and manufacturing process.
  • Peak stress is the maximum unaveraged quadrature-point von Mises stress; display colors are volume-weighted nodal averages.
  • Sharp corners and fixture edges can have stress singularities. A single mesh does not establish stress convergence.
  • Yield ratio is a linear-elastic indicator, not a safety certification. Physical acceptance requires independent validation.
  • Geometry is an imported triangle mesh. Selectable surfaces are clustered patches, not CAD faces.
  • Displacement interpolation is quadratic (P2).
  • Print stiffness is a homogenized equivalent solid, not an explicit infill mesh or sliced toolpath.
  • Walls and top/bottom skins are estimated from the axis-aligned box of the oriented part.
  • Layering is transversely isotropic: print Z is the weak/layer direction after the supplied rotation.
  • gyroid core factors are illustrative (in-plane 0.82, layer 0.72), not coupon-calibrated.
  • Filament modulus, strength and density remain user inputs. No printed-specimen calibration is claimed.
  • Additional raster-scale anisotropy, local voids, bonding defects and moisture are not modelled.
