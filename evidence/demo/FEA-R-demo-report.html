Application notes

Synthetic demonstration only. Rear X=0 is fully fixed; total [0,0,-100] N is uniformly distributed on X=80. Bores are unloaded. No fillets, contact, bolts or preload. Check actual dimensions, material condition and mesh convergence before real use.

Assumptions and interpretation

  • Single homogeneous isotropic solid; quadratic tetrahedral small-strain, linear-elastic static analysis.
  • Fixtures are ideal fully fixed faces. Forces are distributed uniformly across the selected mesh faces in global XYZ.
  • No contact, bolt preload, plasticity, buckling, fatigue, thermal effects or manufacturing anisotropy.
  • Material presets are illustrative inputs. Verify properties for the actual alloy, condition and manufacturing process.
  • Peak stress is the maximum unaveraged quadrature-point von Mises stress; display colors are volume-weighted nodal averages.
  • Sharp corners and fixture edges can have stress singularities. A single mesh does not establish stress convergence.
  • Yield ratio is a linear-elastic indicator, not a safety certification. Physical acceptance requires independent validation.
  • Geometry originated from an image proposal. User confirmation does not constitute metrology.
  • The image is synthetic; all dimensions are supplied demonstration specifications in mm.
  • 90° bracket: base occupies X=0–80, Y=0–40, Z=0–8; upright occupies X=0–8, Y=0–40, Z=0–60. Height is overall.
  • Both bores are plain Ø8 mm through-holes: base center X=57.6, Y=20, axis Z; upright center Y=20, Z=43.2, axis X.
  • No hidden features or fillets; the legs form one connected solid.
  • 7 mm is the initial mesh size. Sharp internal-corner and fixture-edge stresses may be mesh-dependent; assess mesh convergence.
  • Material properties are illustrative, not certified. Analysis assumes homogeneous isotropic small-strain linear elasticity and a static load.
